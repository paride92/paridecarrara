<p>A guest lecture for the PhD Programme in Political and Social Sciences on measuring policy positions from political text, moving from bag-of-words approaches to modern large language models. Using Italian parliamentary speeches as a running example, the tutorial covers text-as-data and bag-of-words, scaling methods (Wordscores and Wordfish), Latent Semantic Scaling, generative language models via OLLAMA, and BERT-based models including ManifestoBerta.</p>
